## Appendix A: The Complete 2023–2027 General Question Pool

This appendix is the complete 2023–2027 General question pool: all 423 active questions, exactly as the NCVEC Question Pool Committee published them, each with its four choices and the keyed answer. The NCVEC released this pool into the public domain, so it is reproduced here verbatim, wording and punctuation included. **It is valid for every General exam given from 2023-07-01 through 2027-06-30 — this pool expires on 2027-06-30.** The next General pool arrives for the exam cycle beginning 2027-07-01; when it does, the swap is contained — the new pool is ingested, this appendix and the chapters' Exam Focus picks are refreshed against it, and the teaching chapters stand. The text here already incorporates all six errata issued for this pool, through the sixth on 2026-02-04, with no seventh errata as of 2026-07-24. The nine questions withdrawn along the way (G1A04, G1C08, G1C09, G1C10, G1E09, G6B09, G8C01, G9C06, and G9D13) simply do not appear, exactly as the official document omits them — they were withdrawn without renumbering, so a few groups skip a number, but no active question is missing.

How to use it: every group of questions maps to one chapter of this book — read that chapter first, then come back and drill its questions here. Each entry shows the question and choices verbatim, then the correct answer with a one-line plain-language why naming the chapter that teaches it. Beneath each entry, the Published ID line carries the question's official answer letter and Part 97 rule reference exactly as printed in the pool document — including a few small printing quirks in the original, preserved rather than repaired: G1D12's misprinted citation `[97.507]`, G1B05's malformed `[97.111((5)(b)]`, and a stray trailing space on G9C01's ID line.

The exam itself draws 35 questions from this pool — one from each of the 35 groups — and 26 correct answers pass. Five questions in group G7A refer to the pool's one official graphic, Figure G7-1; it is redrawn here as an original diagram, embedded with the questions that use it. When you are ready to test yourself, the book's practice page draws a valid 35-question practice test — one question per group — from this same pool.
